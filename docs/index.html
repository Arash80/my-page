About / 04

A researcher’s curiosity.
An engineer’s discipline.

I’m Arash, an Applied AI and Machine Learning Engineer based in Montreal. My work brings together practical software development and research in trustworthy AI, reinforcement learning, and control.

I’m interested in the decisions around a model as much as the model itself: how it is evaluated, how it becomes a service, and how its behavior can be understood.
